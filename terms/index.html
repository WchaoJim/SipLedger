A personal drink journal

SipLedger helps you record drinks, consumed amounts, nutrition values, and their sources. It is a general self-recording tool intended for adults.

Numbers may come from your own entries or automatic label recognition. Check the package text and units. Unknown values remain unknown. Drink records are not a complete record of your diet.

The app does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication, insulin-dosing, allergy-safety, or individual fluid-intake advice. Follow qualified healthcare guidance for your personal dietary or drinking requirements.

Free use and SipLedger Pro

Core manual logging, your existing history, record editing and deletion, JSON backup and restore, and optional drink-record iCloud sync are available without Pro. The free plan also includes a limited number of label scans and favorites.

SipLedger Pro is one non-consumable lifetime purchase. It unlocks unlimited label scans, unlimited favorites, and 90-day trends with monthly comparison. It is not a monthly or annual subscription and does not automatically renew.

“Lifetime” describes the permanent Pro entitlement for these features. It does not promise perpetual operation of Apple services or include every separate product or future service.

The App Store displays the applicable price and currency before you confirm a purchase. Storefront, availability, and Apple’s purchase process determine the transaction; app language does not determine currency.

Use Restore Purchases in the Pro screen to check an existing purchase. A refund or loss of entitlement can affect access to new Pro actions; it does not delete your existing drink records or favorites. Refund requests are handled through Apple’s refund process, subject to applicable consumer rights.

Your records and optional services

You choose what to record and what to share. Keep backups of information that matters to you and check the scope of each backup. iCloud sync, device backups, and exported JSON files cover different things.

iCloud and App Store functions depend on Apple services, account access, connectivity, and available storage. Read the Support & Data Safety page for current sync, image, favorite, and deletion limits.

The Privacy Policy explains how information is handled.
